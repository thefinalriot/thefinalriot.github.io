We respect your privacy and handle your data responsibly:

• We may retain certain data for longer periods where required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention.

7. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) regarding your personal information.

Your CCPA Rights:

• Right to Know: You have the right to request that we disclose what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell about you. You may request this information for the 12-month period preceding your request.

• Right to Delete: You have the right to request that we delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain exceptions (such as where we need the information to complete a transaction or comply with a legal obligation).

• Right to Opt-Out of Sale: Leaper does not sell your personal information to third parties. However, you have the right to direct us not to sell your personal information at any time.

• Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights. We will not deny you services, charge you different prices, or provide a different level of service because you exercised your rights.

• Right to Correct: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.

How to Submit a CCPA Request:

To exercise your CCPA rights, you may contact us at:

privacy@leaper.app

Please include "CCPA Request" in the subject line and specify which right(s) you wish to exercise. We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days. If we need more time, we will inform you of the reason and extension period in writing.

We are required to verify your identity before processing your request. We will use the information you provide to verify your identity against the information we have on file.

Authorized Agent: You may designate an authorized agent to make a CCPA request on your behalf. We may require written proof of the agent's permission to act on your behalf and may verify your identity directly.
